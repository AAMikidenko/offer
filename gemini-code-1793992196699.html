How to attract local guests in an era of AI-overloaded social media

The Market Shift

Social media is flooded with artificial AI content and overly polished ads — and people are tired of it. Today, there is a massive surge in demand for "real, raw content": unedited, authentic, human, and imperfectly real.

1

Who Are Our Customers?

  • Local residents, families, and groups of friends looking for great food right in their neighborhood.
  • People tired of fake corporate ads who want to support real local spots with soul and quality.
  • Local offices and teams ordering lunches, family trays, and catering.
2

What Do They Want?

  • Fresh, hot, delicious food made from honest ingredients.
  • To see who is actually making their food and how clean the kitchen is.
  • To trust the owner and feel the genuine energy behind the establishment.
3

What Do They Want to See?

  • The Owner in Action: how you cook, your personal story, why you love what you do, how you started, and behind-the-scenes of your biggest orders.
  • Real Kitchen Processes: prepping fresh ingredients, grilling, baking, plating — raw and unscripted.
  • The Team: real faces, daily kitchen rhythm, and genuine hospitality.
